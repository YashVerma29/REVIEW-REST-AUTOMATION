Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How does ReviewPilot AI connect to our Google Business reviews?

ReviewPilot AI utilizes official viaSocket Connect integration. You click 'Connect' in the dashboard, log in with your Google account, and your reviews sync automatically. Your credentials remain securely managed and are never stored on client devices.

Will the AI response generator admit fault or promise free meals?

No. ReviewPilot AI has strict non-liability safeguards hardcoded into its prompts and output validators. It crafts empathetic, professional responses without admitting legal liability, promising refunds, or making unauthorized financial commitments.

How does ReviewPilot AI protect tenant data between restaurant owners?

Data isolation is enforced at both the API and database levels. Every query filters strictly by organization_id. Tenant A can never view reviews, tasks, or outlet data belonging to Tenant B.

Can we import review exports from Zomato, Swiggy, or our POS?

Yes. The platform includes an intelligent CSV import engine with custom column mapping. You can upload any standard spreadsheet export, map your columns, and ReviewPilot AI will validate and analyze each row.

Does the AI understand bilingual reviews (e.g., Hinglish or colloquial feedback)?

Yes. ReviewPilot AI detects colloquial phrases (e.g., "khana thanda tha", "poore bag me tel beh gaya"), maps them to standard English operational categories, and alerts your kitchen in real time.
